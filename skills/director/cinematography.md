# Directing grammar

Every visual choice has a purpose, a physical description and a review test. These are editable vocabularies, not a style preset.

## Coverage and composition
| Choice | Story use | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Extreme wide / establishing | Geography, scale, solitude | Subject disappears at phone size |
| Wide / relation | Blocking, distance, power | Unclear eyelines or screen direction |
| Medium | Playable gesture and context | Repeating coverage without a new beat |
| Close / reaction | Decision and consequence | Cutting on every lyric or facial identity drift |
| Detail / insert | A meaningful clue or rhythmic accent | Decorative filler without a payoff |
| Negative space | Absence or off-screen anticipation | Arbitrary empty framing |
| Frame within frame | Constraint or observation | Foreground hides the action |
| Symmetry | Ritual, control, a deliberate pattern | Dead center used out of habit |
| Deep staging | Relationships at several distances | Equal emphasis on every plane |

Keep eyelines and motion direction coherent across continuous cuts. Establish the action axis before changing camera side; bridge a side change with a neutral view or visible crossing. Avoid a nearly identical angle/size cut unless a jump is intended. Match-on-action carries a gesture across the cut. A motivated insert can hide an intentional time compression. These are checks, not inviolable laws: record deliberate violations.

## Camera vocabulary
| Behavior | Physical contract | Narrative job |
|---|---|---|
| locked | Position and orientation fixed | Observation, tension, a baseline |
| push_in / pull_out | Camera translates along a declared path | Realization / separation |
| lateral_track / follow | Translate beside / behind staged movement | Reveal relationships / carry action |
| pan / tilt | Rotate from a fixed pivot | Reveal horizontally / vertically |
| arc / orbit | Declare center, radius, direction and limited angle | Reveal a changed relationship |
| crane | Translate vertically along a feasible path | Release, scale or new geography |
| handheld | Bounded motivated operator motion | Physical immediacy, not random jitter |
| rack_focus | Focus plane changes, camera may stay locked | Transfer attention without a cut |
| zoom | Focal length changes, viewpoint stays fixed | Deliberate optical emphasis |

A digital scale animation is not a dolly: it cannot recover parallax or reveal hidden surfaces. Camera pan is not lateral translation. Specify easing and limits, not "epic motion". Favor one dominant behavior; describe focus separately. Motion blur, cloth/hair inertia, foot contact, gravity and acceleration must agree with the action. Reserve aggressive moves for readable beats and simplify around mouth-sync or detailed hands.

## Optics, light and materials
Choose perspective by purpose: wide for spatial relationships, normal for embodied action, restrained telephoto for isolation, macro for tactile detail. Declare focus target and depth relationships. Lens naming alone does not create correct perspective in a flat plate. A 180-degree shutter impression is a starting point, not a required engine setting; departures need a reason and visible testing.

Name actual key/fill/rim/practical sources, direction, softness and falloff. Preserve highlight texture and readable shadow detail; do not use a blanket teal-orange wash as "cinema". Protect skin before pushing palette. Use grain, bloom, haze and flare only when motivated and restrained. Surface detail belongs to the world: woven cloth, scuffed metal, damp glass, pores, imperfect reflections. Do not add generic dirt to every scene.

## Prompt templates
Still: one moment; subject + singular action; setting; foreground/midground/background; scale/angle/orientation; perspective and focus; sources/exposure; materials; continuity anchors; relevant exclusions.
Motion: local time interval; framing; one camera path with endpoints/easing; one primary subject action; physical response; focus behavior; end state; motivated cut/hold.

## Pacing and sound
Use contrast: stillness before release, wide after a tight run, a reaction after a decision. A beat is a candidate cut, not a compulsory cut. Match strong action or reveal to the phrase/energy change; allow silence, breath and aftermath where the story needs them. Preserve the supplied audio master; do not promise invented native audio or lip-sync support.

## Repair matrix
Identity drift -> simplify action and strengthen concrete reference anchors.
Unclear geography -> return to a relation/wide shot and declare the axis.
Plastic surfaces -> fix light, exposure and specific material behavior, not quality tags.
Weightless motion -> reduce camera complexity and state contacts/inertia.
Repetitive edit -> reallocate coverage globally; changing adjectives is not a fix.
Broken continuity -> compare last/first frames and recorded states, repair one boundary.
Unreadable text -> inspect actual frame at final delivery size; fix explicit coordinates.
