# Parity backlog: EffectCraft and FilmCraft

What [EffectCraft](https://github.com/storytold/effectcraft) (After Effects class) and
[FilmCraft](https://github.com/storytold/filmcraft) (Premiere class) do that kimo does not yet do.
Both are Apache-2.0. kimo implements these itself for its own model; where their code is ported, the
file keeps their notice and says what changed.

Sources: their `docs/effects.md` (308 effects), `docs/target-app-parity.md`, `docs/gaps.md` and READMEs,
compared with kimo's `list_effects` (189 kinds) and tool registry (about 575 tools) on 2026-11-23.

Order agreed: effects first, GPU and codecs last.

`[x]` done, `[ ]` to do, `(n)` = kimo batch that delivered it.

## 1. Effects

From batch 7, EffectCraft's own effect code runs inside kimo: `crates/kimo-ec` vendors their effect modules
(MIT OR Apache-2.0, notice kept, re-generated by a script from a checkout) and `kimo-core/src/ec_bridge.rs` lists
each one as a kimo effect kind. Batch 7 brought 116 of their effects in this way (all blur, channel, colour, distort,
generate, keying, noise, perspective, simulation, stylize, transition and utility effects in the vendored modules);
the boxes below marked (7) came from it.

Batch 8 vendored 17 more of their modules (174 effects bridged in all) and gave the bridge host services: a layer
input takes a clip id in `strings` (e.g. `difference_layer`), and time effects (Echo, Timewarp, Pixel Motion Blur,
Time Displacement...) read the clip's own frames at other times. Keylight is registered as Screen Color Key (their
naming). Batch 9 vendored their tracker and segmentation crates (pure Rust: KLT, planar and camera solves, stabilizer,
graph-cut segmentation, MobileSAM and MediaPipe inference), bringing Object Matte (click point or box; works with the
built-in graph-cut engine, no download), Warp Stabilizer, 3D Camera Tracker and Face Track (180 effects bridged).
Still missing: effects that need EffectCraft's property tree (Puppet, Paint, Roto Brush strokes), its text engine
(text effects) and its audio renderer (audio effects); MobileSAM weights are not wired to kimo's model downloader yet; 3D channel effects run but kimo layers carry no depth or ID channels yet.

kimo already has many After Effects effects under its own names (blur, glow, curves, levels, keyer,
choker, wave, ripple, twirl, bulge, polar, offset, motion_tile, four_color_gradient, fractal_noise,
caustics, cc_glass, cc_kaleida, lens_flare, light_rays, write_on, audio_spectrum, posterize, threshold,
find_edges, emboss, mosaic, scatter, roughen_edges, gradient_ramp, fill, stroke, drop_shadow,
outer_glow, spill_suppress, luma_key, chroma_key, ...). These are the ones still missing.
